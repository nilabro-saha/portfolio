Academic work

Five projects and seminar reviews at the intersection of mechanism theory, simulation, and robotics.

CSIR-CMERI / B.Tech. thesis / February–May 2022

Underactuated hand exoskeleton

Analysed a five-digit, six-actuator system for master-slave grasping and force feedback. MATLAB kinematics and linkage optimization extended the modeled MCP range to approximately 110°; MSC Adams identified interference near 60° of index-MCP flexion, limiting the assembly’s usable range.

16-link, 3-DOF finger mechanisms · 45° finger-PIP and 50° thumb flexion targets · 23–28 mm actuator travel

Advisors: Prof. Shibendu Shekhar Roy, NIT Durgapur; Dr. Abhijit Mahapatra, CSIR-CMERI.

Code ↗ Zenodo Article ↗

IIT Delhi Robotics Lab / May 2020–May 2022

Mechanisms, made interactive

Extended MechAnalyzer with 11 interactive 3D models, including gear trains, straight-line mechanisms, and six- and eight-bar linkages. Added modules for involute gear meshing and the history of mechanism theory.

C# · OpenGL · Coupler curves · Kinematic analysis

Supervised by Prof. Subir Kumar Saha, IIT Delhi, and Prof. Rajeevlochana G. Chittawadigi, Amrita School of Engineering, Bengaluru.

YouTube Presentation ↗

INAE / CSIR-CMERI / May–July 2021

Parallel-kinematic system modelling

Derived a six-state, two-input model of an ultra-precision coordinate measuring stage and evaluated four vertical/lateral loading cases. Separately designed an LQR controller for a four-state cart-pendulum benchmark and explored Adams–Simulink co-simulation; the measuring-stage controller remained future work.

140–170 N vertical / 0–20 N lateral loads · MATLAB/Simulink · MSC Adams

Mentored by Dr. N. C. Murmu, CSIR-CMERI, through the INAE engineering student menteeship.
